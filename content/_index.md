I am an Assistant Professor of Political Science at Yale University, where I study American politics, elections, and the media.

Much of my research is on the political economy of the media: what shapes the information news outlets give voters about candidates and elections, and how that information affects how people vote. I also study how redistricting rules shape representation, and I build datasets and methods for studying elections.

I am a Resident Fellow at the Institution for Social and Policy Studies (ISPS) and a Faculty Affiliate at the Center for the Study of American Politics (CSAP), both at Yale. During the 2025-2026 academic year I was a Visiting Assistant Professor at MIT and a Faculty Affiliate of the MIT Election Data and Science Lab. Previously I was affiliated with the Center for American Political Studies (CAPS) and the Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS) at Harvard, and I worked for the MIT Election Data and Science Lab (MEDSL) and the Stanford-MIT Healthy Elections Project.

I can be reached at kevin.deluca@yale.edu.
