## Derived Estimates

### Candidate Quality Estimates

I develop measures of candidate quality using local newspaper endorsements as a signal of candidate viability and electoral strength. These estimates are derived from endorsement patterns across U.S. elections and provide researchers with a quantitative measure of candidate quality that can be used in election forecasting and analysis.

**How they are estimated:** Candidate quality estimates are constructed using endorsement data from local newspapers, leveraging the idea that newspapers endorse candidates they perceive as higher quality. The methodology accounts for newspaper partisanship and editorial preferences to isolate the underlying quality signal.

**How to cite:** If you use these candidate quality estimates in your research, please cite:

> DeLuca, Kevin. "Editor's Choice: Measuring Candidate Quality using Local Newspaper Endorsements." *Journal of Politics*, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1086/736463

### Partisan Bias and Slant Estimates

These estimates measure the partisan bias and editorial slant of local newspapers based on their endorsement patterns. The estimates quantify the degree to which newspapers systematically favor Democratic or Republican candidates, providing a measure of media bias at the local level.

**How they are estimated:** Partisan bias estimates are derived by analyzing the partisan composition of endorsed candidates relative to the local electorate. The methodology uses Bayesian inference to account for variation in electoral competitiveness and endorsement frequency across newspapers and elections.

**How to cite:** If you use these partisan bias estimates in your research, please cite:

> DeLuca, Kevin. "Measuring Candidate Quality using Local Newspaper Endorsements." *Journal of Politics*, 2025. https://doi.org/10.1086/736463

## Newspaper Endorsements Data

### Overview

The newspaper endorsements dataset contains comprehensive records of editorial endorsements for political candidates from U.S. local newspapers. This data has been used to study candidate quality, media bias, and the political influence of local news outlets.

### Data Description

The dataset includes:
- Endorsement records from local newspapers across multiple election cycles
- Information on endorsed candidates, offices, and election dates
- Newspaper identifiers and geographic information
- Partisan affiliation of endorsed candidates

### Usage

This data has been used in multiple research projects to:
- Develop candidate quality measures
- Analyze partisan bias in local media
- Study the relationship between endorsements and election outcomes
- Examine media influence on electoral behavior

### Access the Data

The newspaper endorsements dataset is hosted on GitHub and can be accessed here: [newspaper_endorsements repository](https://github.com/kevin-deluca-polisci/newspaper_endorsements/tree/main)

You can download the most recent version of the data directly from the GitHub repository.

### How to Cite

> DeLuca, Kevin. "American Election Results at the Precinct Level." *Scientific Data*, vol. 9, no. 651, 2022. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01745-0

For detailed information about data collection, methodology, and structure, please refer to the [README](https://github.com/kevin-deluca-polisci/newspaper_endorsements/tree/main) in the GitHub repository.
