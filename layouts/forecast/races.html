Nothing here knows who the candidates are, which is exactly why it disagrees with the professionals where a party has an unusually strong recruit.

Why there is no House table here

The House model exists and runs daily. It is not published, and the reason is arithmetic rather than caution.

District forecasts are built from a licensed partisan index. Given the national tide, which we do publish, that index is exactly recoverable from any district forecast:

PVI = (district margin − national tide) ÷ 2

So publishing 435 district margins would republish the index itself. The district run writes to a gitignored directory and stays there. This becomes publishable the day we hold a district-level presidential baseline we are allowed to redistribute, which is a licensing question rather than a modelling one.
