Why so many districts share a margin. Every district margin is the same national tide plus twice that district’s lean, and the lean is carried at low resolution, so the margins land on a coarse grid: 435 districts take only {{ len $L0.seats }} drawn values, and a dozen or more can share one. It is arithmetic rather than a bug, but it has a consequence worth knowing: inside a tied group the seat ORDER is arbitrary, so the district named at the majority line is one of several that could equally have been named. Read it as “the majority is decided around here”, not as a call on that district. How the mapping works.
